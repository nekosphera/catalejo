# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a professional, inclusive and technically rigorous community. Participants must treat others with respect, evaluate ideas on evidence and make room for different backgrounds and communication styles.

## Expected behaviour

- Give specific, constructive feedback.
- Distinguish facts, assumptions, compatibility claims and certification.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, intellectual property and applicable law.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and deliberately disruptive conduct.
- Disclose conflicts of interest relevant to a contribution or review.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the repository owner through the contact method shown on the owner's GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and proportionately. Maintainers may edit, reject or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.
